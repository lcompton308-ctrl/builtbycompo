Refund policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Draft. Highlighted parts still need your decision, and this page should be checked by a solicitor or a fixed-fee legal service before you rely on it.

Website builds

  • You pay half to start and half when your site is ready to go live.
  • Deposit: [TO DECIDE: for example, refundable in full until the agreed start date, and not refundable once work has started]
  • If you cancel part-way through, you pay for the work done up to that point. If that's less than you've already paid, I refund the difference.
  • The final payment isn't refundable once your site has gone live.

If something isn't right

For 30 days after launch, if anything doesn't work the way we agreed, I'll fix it free of charge. Please tell me first and give me the chance to put it right.

Monthly plans

  • Your first Care plan has a 3-month minimum. After that it runs month to month.
  • You can cancel with 30 days' notice by email or WhatsApp.
  • Monthly payments aren't refunded for part-used months.

Costs paid to other companies

Domain names, email accounts and other services you buy from another company are covered by that company's own refund rules.

Your legal rights

My services are for businesses. If you're buying as a consumer, meaning not for your trade, business or profession, you may have extra rights, including a 14-day right to cancel a service you ordered at a distance. Nothing in this policy takes away any legal rights you have.

How to ask for a refund

Email luke@builtbycompo.co.uk with your name and project. I'll reply within 5 working days, and any refund due is paid within 14 days to the account you paid from.
