Things you might ask.

What projects do you take on?

Websites for small businesses, sole traders and local services. That could be a simple five-page site or something with custom tools, like a quiz or a booking helper.

If you're not sure whether your idea fits, ask me. I'll tell you straight if I'm not the right person for it.

How much does a project cost?

My pricing page shows the starting price for each type of build and for the monthly plans.

Bespoke projects vary, so those prices depend on what you need. Take a look at the pricing page or start a conversation, and I'll give you one fixed price before anything begins.

How involved do I have to be?

Not very. I need your knowledge of your business, the facts and your feedback. I do the rest.

You'll get a private preview link while I build, and two rounds of changes are included.

When can you start, and how long will it take?

It depends on the size of the project and what I already have booked in. Once we've talked, I'll give you an accurate start date and timeframe in writing with your quote.

The clock starts when I have your wording and photos, so having those ready is the quickest way to a finished site.

Do you design and build the website?

Yes. I design it, build it, host it and look after it. One person from start to finish.

On a care plan I also run a health check every month and make your updates for you, so the site keeps running well.
