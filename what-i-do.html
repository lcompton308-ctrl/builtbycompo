SEO set-up

SEO is short for search engine optimisation. It's the behind-the-scenes work that helps Google understand your site and show it to people searching for what you do.

  • Page titles and descriptions written the way people search
  • Your site registered with Google, so new pages get picked up
  • Fast, tidy pages, which Google rewards
  • Help with your Google Business Profile, so you show up on Maps
  • A proper preview image when your link is shared

Hosting and care

  • You own your domain, your accounts and your finished site
