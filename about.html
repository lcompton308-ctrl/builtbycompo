Hello, I'm Compo.

A small studio based in Essex, England

Built by Compo is me, a laptop and a lot of coffee. Compo has been my nickname for years, and it stuck.

I make websites for small businesses that want something better than a template. I design each one from scratch, build it by hand and set it up to be found on Google. Then I host it and look after it, so you never have to log in to anything.

I like things that are simple, quick and a bit different. I'll tell you plainly what I think will work, and I'll always give you a fixed price before I start.

When I'm not building websites, I'm usually out walking Lemon, my chihuahua. She's in charge of morale.
