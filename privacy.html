Who I am

Built by Compo is the trading name of Luke Compton, a sole trader based in Essex, England. Email: luke@builtbycompo.co.uk. I'm the "controller" of your personal information, which means I decide how it's used.

What I collect, and why

The contact form on this site does not send or store anything. It only prepares a message in your own email or WhatsApp app.

Who I share it with

I never sell your information. I use a small number of trusted services to run the business, and they only process information on my instructions or as independent providers:

  • Google Workspace, for email and documents
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message me there
  • GitHub, which hosts this website
  • Cloudflare, which manages my domain name

Some of these companies store information outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the UK–US Data Bridge or standard contract clauses.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: up to 12 months
  • Client records and invoices: 6 years after our work ends, for tax and legal reasons

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of your information, ask me to correct or delete it, ask me to limit how I use it, or object to me using it. To do any of these, email luke@builtbycompo.co.uk and I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies and doesn't save anything on your device. It has no advertising or tracking tools, which is why you don't see a cookie banner.
